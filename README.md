## 📌 Problem Statement
As digital payment adoption (UPI, NetBanking) expands rapidly across Tier-2, Tier-3, and rural India, non-tech-savvy and vernacular-speaking citizens are increasingly targeted by sophisticated cyber scams. Phishing messages, utility bill cutoff threats, fake digital arrest extortions, and rogue APK links are predominantly delivered in regional Indian languages (Hindi, Hinglish, Marathi, Tamil, Bengali, etc.). Standard cybersecurity tools and web filters are overwhelmingly optimized for English text, leaving millions vulnerable to financial theft and identity impersonation.



---

## 💡 Solution Overview
**Kavach AI** is an intelligent digital trust and regional security system designed to protect non-English speaking citizens from digital fraud. Powered by the **Gemini 1.5 Flash API**, Kavach AI parses localized scripts, analyzes psychological threat intent, extracts malicious links from screenshots, and delivers real-time **Vernacular Audio Warnings** so elderly and non-literate users can hear the safety assessment.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **🌐 Multilingual Threat Parser:** Supports 8+ Indian languages including Hinglish, Hindi (Devanagari), Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, and Kannada.
- **⚡ Multimodal Input Analysis:**
  - **SMS / Text Messages:** Detects urgency tactics, fake job traps, and extortion threats.
  - **UPI / Web Links:** Identifies fake payment gateways, QR collect scams, and phishing domains.
  - **Screenshot OCR:** Extracts text from WhatsApp screenshots and bill notices using Gemini Vision capabilities.
  - **Call Audio Scans:** Processes call recordings to identify voice scam patterns and impersonations.
- **🔊 Vernacular Text-to-Speech (TTS):** Automatically speaks out the safety warning in the user's native language/Hinglish.
- **🚨 Direct Helpline Escalation:** One-tap action to copy fraud details and report directly to the **1930 Cyber Crime Helpline**.
- **⚖️ Responsible AI Disclaimer:** Includes clear predictive assessment markers to prevent user over-reliance on automated AI outputs.
